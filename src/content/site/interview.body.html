    Run a spoken mock

    The agentic-interview skill turns any Claude chat into an interviewer. It uses the same 18 scenarios and the same checklist as the drills, then scores you on seven areas.

    1. Open a new Claude chat. On your phone you can switch to voice mode.
    2. Say or paste one of the openers below.
    3. Answer the interviewer's questions. Say hint, skip or stop at any time.
    4. At the end you get a scorecard, three fixes with links to the right modules, and a result block.

    If the chat does not pick up the skill, start with “use the agentic-interview skill” and then give the opener.

    Openers

    mock interview, senior, broking
    quick mock, SDE-2, UPI
    full mock, staff, surprise me

    Levels: SDE-2, Senior (default), Staff. Length: Quick, about 15 minutes, or Full, about 45 minutes.
